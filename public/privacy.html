Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Commento is a personal tool developed by Yannick Pulver to manage Instagram comments. This policy explains how your data is handled.

2. Data We Access

When you connect your Instagram account, we request access to:

  • Your Instagram Business/Creator account profile
  • Comments on your posts
  • Ability to reply to comments on your behalf

3. Data Storage

We do NOT store:

  • Your Instagram password
  • Your comments or post content on our servers
  • Any personal data beyond what's needed for authentication

Authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser/session only.

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses the official Instagram Graph API provided by Meta. Your use is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Deletion

You can revoke access at any time through your Instagram settings under "Apps and Websites". This will remove all access immediately.

6. Contact

Questions? Contact: hey@yannickpulver.com
